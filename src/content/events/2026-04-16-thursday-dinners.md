Thursday night dinners are back at Croydon Park Oval for the 2026 season. After training, head up to the clubrooms for a feed, a beer and the weekend team selections.

**When:** Thursday nights after training
**Where:** Croydon Park Oval Clubrooms
**Cost:** $20 per head

On the menu this week:

- Meatball subs, salad and chips
- Hot jam donuts and ice cream for dessert

Team selections for the weekend are announced on the night. A good feed, a good atmosphere and the first look at who's running out on Saturday. See you there.
