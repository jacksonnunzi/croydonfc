Round 1 at Croydon Park Oval couldn't have gone much better for the Blues. All three Croydon football sides - Under 19.5s, Reserves and Seniors - got the job done against Lilydale on home turf, putting the club on the board in Division 2 of the Eastern Football Netball League with a clean sweep.

## The day in numbers

- **Under 19.5** - Croydon 11.14 (80) def Lilydale 3.7 (25). Croydon U19.5 won by **55 points**.
- **Reserves** - Croydon 9.7 (61) def Lilydale 3.6 (24). Croydon Reserves won by **37 points**.
- **Seniors** - Croydon 6.9 (45) def Lilydale 4.4 (28). Croydon Seniors won by **17 points**.

A big thanks to everyone who came down to support the club on Round 1. The kiosk was humming, the under 19.5s set the tone early, and the reserves and seniors carried that energy through the rest of the day.

## Next up

- **Round 2 - Saturday 18 April** - Seniors and Reserves travel to HE Parker Reserve to take on Heathmont.
- **Under 19.5** - bye this week, back in action Round 3 (ANZAC Round) at East Burwood.

Come down, get around the boys and support the Blues. Up the Blues!
